FloodSAR BD - Sylhet 2022 web tiles

Folder:
  tiles/<layer-id>/<z>/<x>/<y>.png

Tile scheme: XYZ
Display CRS: EPSG:3857
Zoom range: 6-13

Layers are listed in manifest.json.

The source COGs are in EPSG:32646 and have been
reprojected by GDAL during tile generation.

Interpretation:
- Candidate gain layers are exploratory transitions.
- GFM is a satellite-derived reference classification.
- ALOS-2 polygons are positive detections only.
- ML predictions are not independently verified flood extent.
